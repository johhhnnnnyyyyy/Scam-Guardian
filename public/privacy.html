Privacy notice

Notice version 2026-11-10. This is an MVP notice prepared for review; it is not legal advice and must be checked by counsel before public use.

What Guardian is for

Guardian listens to a phone call on speakerphone, through a second device you place nearby, and warns you if the call sounds like a known scam. Your family members can see reason-only warnings if you choose to share.

What is processed, and why

  • Call audio while you press Listen: streamed to our speech-recognition provider (Gnani, India) to produce text. Guardian does not store audio.
  • Call text: checked by Guardian's rules and by an AI model to find scam tactics. Kept in memory only for the active session (at most 60 minutes) and never written to disk or shown to family.
  • Your voice measurement, made once from a 12-second reading, so Guardian can tell your voice from the caller's. Only derived numbers are kept, never the recording. It improves slowly from calls where Guardian is certain it heard you.
  • Warning timeline: time, level and reasons (for example "asked for a secret code"), plus how you or your family labelled each warning.
  • Family accounts: username and a protected passphrase hash; device credentials stored only as hashes.

The other person on the call

Guardian also hears the caller. It does not identify callers, does not store their voice or words after the session, and does not share them with anyone. Only use Guardian on calls you take yourself.

Who receives data

Gnani (speech recognition and spoken warnings, India) and the configured AI model provider (call text only). Linked family members receive reason-only warnings when you turn sharing on.

How long it is kept

Session text: until the session ends (at most 60 minutes). Timeline: the latest 30 warnings. Voice measurement and settings: until you delete them. Unused device sign-ins expire after 30 days (90 days at most).

Your choices and rights

  • Download my data and Delete everything are under More → "Your information". Deleting removes your timeline, settings and voice measurement, and unlinks family accounts.
  • Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it: stop listening, delete your voice measurement, or delete everything.
  • Family members can delete their own account from the family page.
  • Questions or complaints: contact the operator of this service (grievance contact to be added before public launch).

Age

Guardian is for adults. Voice enrollment asks you to confirm you are 18 or older.

If you lose money

Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately, or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Suspected fraud calls can also be reported through Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu).
